Alternative 3'-terminal exon 3' of gene

Detailed expression analysis of this fictive gene showed that a minor fraction of the transcripts (<5%) terminated at an alternative 3'-terminal exon located downstream of the normal 3'-terminla exon (exon 55). Based on a coding DNA reference sequence, numbering of these differentially spliced nucleotides is straightforward, i.e. as for nucleotides downstream of the translation termination codon c.*488 to c.*784. The encoded amino acids can best be numbered p.Gly*1 to p.Gln*13; this number clearly indicates their position in relation to the other amino acids with the asterisk (*) number indicating a location downstream of the normal translation termination codon (see below).
